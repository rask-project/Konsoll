## What's new

3.7.0 keeps Konsoll up to date by itself.

### Updates

- **Automatic updates.** Once a day Konsoll looks for a newer release,
  downloads it in the background and installs it when you quit. Updates are
  signed, and they open without clearing the quarantine flag.
- **Konsoll ▸ Check for Updates…** checks right away, shows what changed and
  offers to install and relaunch.
- **Settings ▸ Updates** turns the automatic checks and downloads off and
  picks the channel: **Stable only** (the default) or **Stable and beta**,
  which also offers pre-releases for testing.

This is the last version to install by hand: from here on, Konsoll updates
itself.
